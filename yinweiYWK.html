About Yinwei (YWK)

A leading high-tech enterprise dedicated to automated, special-purpose industrial sewing machines.

Founded in Hong Kong, Serving the World

Founded in 2006 and headquartered in the Hong Kong region, Yinwei Trade (HK) Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise integrating the R&D, production, sales and service of automated special-purpose industrial sewing machines.

Our mainland China operations are based in Zhongshan City, while a global network of overseas branches spans Taiwan, Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnam), Hai Phong (Vietnam), Cambodia, Bangladesh, India and Myanmar.

Yinwei provides end customers with personalized on-site solutions and custom pattern templates tailored to each production process — solving the real challenges factories face every day.

"Technology is the primary productive force; service is the primary competitive advantage." Yinwei's products and service have earned unanimous praise from customers at home and abroad — including major brands such as Adidas and Nike.
